Privacy policy

Updated 3 November 2026 · Operator: MD. Shawon Hossain

Planora is a personal planning application operated by MD. Shawon Hossain. For privacy questions, access/correction requests, or deletion requests, email shawon12hossain@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, session cookies, reset links, or Google tokens.

This deployment is a staging/pilot service. Public account-email delivery and unrestricted Google connection are not yet enabled. Avoid storing highly sensitive information or using Planora as your only copy of important plans.

Information and purposes

  • Account name, email, password hash, timezone, preferences, categories, verification status, and session/security version are used to authenticate you and manage your account. Recovery and verification tokens are stored as hashes with expiry times.
  • Task titles, notes, schedules, status, recurrence, templates, movement history, and daily outcomes are used to provide planning, reviews, progress, and requested exports.
  • Optional Google connection information, event mappings, and sync results support the integration described below.
  • Reminder device identifiers and delivery claims help prevent duplicate notifications. Administrative actions have audit records. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request metadata, and operational logs.

The current application does not implement advertising, sale of user data, or AI model training with planner or Google data.

Optional Google Calendar connection

Google connection is optional. With your authorization, Planora receives your Google account identifier and email and requests permission to create secondary calendars and manage events on calendars created by the app. It does not request access to all your existing calendars. The requested scopes are OpenID, email, and calendar.app.created.

When you choose to sync, Planora sends relevant task titles, notes, schedule, timezone, and reminder settings to Google Calendar. It stores your connection email, Google identifiers, calendar/event mappings, sync status, and an encrypted refresh token. Access tokens are held temporarily in server memory. It reads event information needed for syncing and avoiding changes to unrelated events.

Planora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used for the user-facing Calendar integration, not advertising or sale.

Disconnect using Planora's Google Calendar controls or revoke access in your Google Account connections. Planora disconnect removes its saved refresh token and stops future sync; mapping/sync metadata may remain. It does not automatically delete previously created Google calendars or events. Remove unwanted remote content in Google Calendar.

Cookies and local storage

Necessary cookies maintain your session, bind a Google authorization attempt to your browser, and temporarily unlock administration when applicable. Browser storage holds display/reminder settings and a reminder device identifier. The PWA caches public build assets, the public offline workspace shell, and a reconnect screen, not API responses or authenticated HTML.

After online sign-in, Planora saves your account-owned planner, templates, recurring rules, and history in IndexedDB, allowing the same interface to work offline. Local changes are queued for synchronization. Anyone with access to that browser profile can read the saved copy without signing in while offline. Use a trusted device. Sign out online after syncing to revoke the session and clear the device copy. Clearing site data, browser eviction, or uninstalling can lose unsynced edits; recovery JSON backups contain private data and remain under your control.

Supported planner changes sync automatically while the app is open and connected, waiting until dialogs close. Conflicts or uncertain prior attempts pause replay rather than silently overwriting newer account data. PDF/CSV/ICS files can be generated locally. Google requests, account emails, password/email changes, fresh sign-in, and administrative actions still require internet. Passwords and Google refresh tokens are not saved in the queue. Closed-app reminders and background sync are not guaranteed.

Providers and access

The current deployment uses Vercel for frontend hosting/proxying, Render for the API, MongoDB Atlas for storage, Resend for account-email attempts, and Google when you enable Calendar integration. Providers process the information needed for their services and may operate outside your country. Account-email requests include the recipient address and a time-limited link. Exported files are saved and shared under your control.

Administrative screens expose account-management metadata and audit records rather than a general task-browsing interface. The operator and authorized infrastructure providers may have technical access needed to maintain storage, respond to support requests, or investigate security incidents. Planora is not end-to-end encrypted.

Retention and your choices

Planner data and history generally remain until removed through the relevant workflow or an account-deletion request. Removing a task from an active view can preserve historical information. Some expired security tokens remain stored but cannot be used; OAuth state and reminder claims have expiry cleanup. Security audit records and provider logs have separate retention. A final backup/retention schedule and self-service account deletion are still being prepared; the pilot does not promise immediate erasure from every backup or provider log.

You can edit your profile, delete tasks/templates, download PDF/CSV/ICS exports, disable reminders, and disconnect Google. For account deletion or information not removable in the interface, contact the email above. The operator will confirm account ownership before handling the request. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional access, correction, deletion, or complaint rights.
