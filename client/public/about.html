Your day, your time.

Planora is a personal planner developed and operated by MD. Shawon Hossain. Plan exact task times, keep a flexible bucket list, reuse templates, schedule recurring tasks, and review progress.

Features

  • Day, week, and month calendar views with exact task times.
  • Recurrence, templates, movement history, daily reviews, and progress.
  • PDF, CSV, and ICS exports.
  • An installable PWA with offline support in the same planner interface, queued edits, local exports, and automatic sync while open and connected.
  • Optional selected-task sync to a dedicated Google calendar.

This is a free staging/pilot service. Google connection is limited to approved testers, account email cannot yet reach arbitrary users, and hosting may pause or take time to wake. Public release preparation is ongoing.

Google Calendar

Connection is optional. Planora requests Google identity/email and permission to create secondary calendars and manage events on calendars created by the app. It uses the connection for task synchronization, not advertising. Read the privacy policy for storage, sharing, and disconnect details.
